# Refund and Cancellation Policy

## Cancelling a subscription
Customers can cancel at any time from **Settings → Billing → Cancel plan**. The workspace stays active until the end of the current billing period. After that it is downgraded to a read-only state for 30 days, and then all data is deleted permanently.

## Refunds for monthly plans
Monthly plans are not refunded for partial months. If a customer was charged by mistake (for example a duplicate charge), the full amount is refunded within **5 business days**.

## Refunds for annual plans
Annual plans can be refunded **within the first 30 days** after purchase for the full amount. After 30 days, a pro-rated refund is possible only if Nimbus Flow failed to meet its SLA for two consecutive months.

## Enterprise contracts
Enterprise contracts follow the terms of the signed agreement. Early termination requires 90 days written notice.

## How to request a refund
Send an email to billing@nimbusflow.example with the workspace ID and invoice number. Refund requests are handled by the finance team, not by technical support.

## Downgrading
Downgrading from Business to Starter takes effect at the next billing date. If the workspace has more than 20 active workflows, the extra workflows are deactivated (not deleted) and the customer chooses which ones stay active.
