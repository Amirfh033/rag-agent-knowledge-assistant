# Support Channels and Service Level Agreement

## Support channels
- **Email:** support@nimbusflow.example (all plans)
- **Live chat:** inside the app, Monday to Friday 08:00–18:00 CET (Business and Enterprise)
- **Phone:** 24/7 hotline for Enterprise customers only
- **Community forum:** community.nimbusflow.example (all users)

## Priority levels
| Priority | Definition | First response (Business) | First response (Enterprise) |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 Critical | Production workflows down, no workaround | 4 business hours | 1 hour, 24/7 |
| P2 High | Major feature broken, workaround exists | 8 business hours | 4 hours |
| P3 Normal | Question or minor bug | 2 business days | 1 business day |

Starter customers receive email support with a first response within 2 business days for all priorities.

## Uptime SLA
Enterprise customers have a **99.9% monthly uptime** guarantee. If uptime falls below that, service credits are applied automatically:
- 99.0% – 99.9%: 10% credit of the monthly fee
- 95.0% – 99.0%: 25% credit
- below 95.0%: 50% credit

Planned maintenance is announced at least **72 hours** in advance on status.nimbusflow.example and does not count as downtime.

## Escalation
If a P1 ticket is not resolved within 8 hours, it is escalated to the on-call engineering lead. Customers can also escalate by replying "ESCALATE" to the ticket email.
