# Customer Onboarding Guide

## Week 1 — Setup
1. The account owner creates the workspace and invites team members from **Settings → Members**.
2. Connect the first integrations. The most used ones are Gmail, Slack, HubSpot, Google Sheets and PostgreSQL.
3. Import one of the 150+ ready-made templates from the template gallery.

## Week 2 — First production workflow
- Pick one repetitive process, for example lead routing or invoice reminders.
- Test with the **Test run** button; test runs do not count towards the execution limit.
- Turn on **error notifications** in workflow settings so failures are emailed to the owner.

## Onboarding call
Business customers get **one free 60-minute onboarding call** with a solutions engineer. Enterprise customers get a dedicated onboarding program of **4 weekly sessions**. Starter customers can book a paid onboarding call for €99.

## Training
The Nimbus Flow Academy offers free video courses. Completing the "Automation Fundamentals" course gives a certificate that can be shared on LinkedIn.

## Health check
After 90 days, Business and Enterprise customers are offered a free workspace health check that reviews error rates, unused workflows and security settings.
