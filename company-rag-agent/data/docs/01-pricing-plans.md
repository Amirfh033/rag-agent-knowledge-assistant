# Nimbus Flow — Pricing and Plans

Nimbus Flow is a workflow-automation platform for small and mid-sized companies. All prices are per month, billed in euros, excluding VAT.

## Plans

### Starter — €29 per month
- Up to 3 users
- 2,000 workflow executions per month
- 20 active workflows
- Email support only, response within 2 business days
- Execution history kept for 7 days

### Business — €149 per month
- Up to 25 users
- 50,000 workflow executions per month
- Unlimited active workflows
- Email and chat support, response within 8 business hours
- Execution history kept for 30 days
- Single sign-on (SSO) with Google and Microsoft
- Role-based access control

### Enterprise — custom price
- Unlimited users and executions
- Dedicated customer success manager
- 24/7 phone support with a 1-hour response time for critical incidents
- 99.9% uptime SLA with service credits
- Execution history kept for 365 days
- Self-hosting option and EU-only data residency
- Contract starts at 12 months; contact sales@nimbusflow.example for a quote

## Annual billing
Customers who pay yearly get **2 months free** (they pay for 10 months instead of 12).

## Extra executions
If a Starter or Business workspace goes over its monthly execution limit, extra executions cost **€5 per additional 1,000 executions**. Workflows are never stopped automatically; the overage is added to the next invoice.

## Discounts
- Registered non-profit organisations and schools get **40% off** Starter and Business.
- Start-ups younger than 2 years with less than €1M funding can apply for the Start-up program: Business plan at 50% off for the first year.

## Free trial
Every new workspace gets a **14-day free trial** of the Business plan. No credit card is required. After the trial the workspace moves to Starter unless a paid plan is chosen.
